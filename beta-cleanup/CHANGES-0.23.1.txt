PLAYBOOK VOCAL STUDIO 0.23.1 — CHANGES (fixes on 0.23.0; beta link only)

1. AUTOSAVE IS DURABLE
- A change made just before a reload, or just before the tab was closed or killed, could be
  lost: the full browser save waited 800 ms and the small backup journal was only written
  when the page closed normally.
- Now the journal (song arrangement: choices, sections, mutes, levels, settings — no audio)
  is written synchronously on every change, and again on beforeunload, freeze and when the
  tab is hidden. It is kept after recovery until the next full save is written. The full
  save waits 250 ms instead of 800 ms. POLISH audio always comes from the full save.
- Test: tests-0.23/durable231.py (real UI, 1366 and 390): change then reload at once; tab
  crashed straight after a change. 0.23.1 6/6; 0.23.0 5/6 (lost the change after a crash).

2. DOWNLOADS BOX NO LONGER COVERS THE CONTROLS
- The "prepared downloads" box is now a small toast. On phones it sits at the bottom, never
  over the transport or the top buttons. After 6 s it folds to a small "⬇ Downloads" chip;
  tap it to see the link again. Test: tests-0.23/downloads231.py (390 and 1366) 8/8.

3. TEST ROBUSTNESS
- t21 at 390 sometimes timed out clicking the status line to give the page keyboard focus
  (the line re-renders). It now focuses the page without a click. 3/3 reruns pass.
