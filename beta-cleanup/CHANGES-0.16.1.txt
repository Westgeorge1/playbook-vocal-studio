Playbook Vocal Studio 0.16.1 - fixes after the 0.16.0 debug and review

Same project format (version 9). 0.16.1 projects open in 0.15 and 0.16.0, and
0.15 / 0.16.0 projects open in 0.16.1. The sound engine is not changed
(phrase-core.js, worker.js, phrase-worker.js, engine-wasm.*, daw.css are
byte-identical to 0.16.0).

FIXED
 1. Laptop screens (1366 x 768): the COMP lane and take lanes 4-5 were hidden
    below the fold of the lanes box. The lanes box now uses the space it has.
 2. After Export, the browser's download box sat on top of
    "Comp done -> Go to POLISH" and blocked the click (laptop) or covered it
    (phone). On the RECORD & COMP screen the download box now sits bottom-left
    on a computer and at the top on a phone.
 3. Dragging a part's IN / OUT flag past the end of its chosen take quietly
    un-chose that take. It still has to (the take does not cover the part any
    more), but now a message says so and Undo puts it back.
 4. Very slow songs (about 50 BPM) with a 2-bar count-in and a part right at
    0:00 could throw the take away ("Invalid take position") when the latency
    setting was about 0.25 s or more. Recording now starts just early enough to
    be safe; the take is kept.
 5. Microphone errors are in plain English (blocked, no microphone found,
    microphone in use by another app) instead of the browser's raw text.
 6. Shift+R on RECORD & COMP now means "next part and record" (as the help
    says). Before it made an extra 8-second "Phrase N" part.
 7. After changing Count-in, R and Space work straight away (the drop-down no
    longer keeps the keyboard).
 8. The 33rd take on one part (or 129th in the project) now gives a clear
    "delete some takes first" message.
 9. Adding a part that overlaps another part now warns you that, where they
    overlap, the newer part's take is used in the comp.
10. While a big saved session is still opening, the panel says
    "Opening your saved session... one moment" instead of "Add your first part".
11. The part chips no longer scroll on every screen update, only when you pick
    a different part, and only sideways.
12. Playing the comp no longer re-copies the whole comp each time (faster
    Play on long songs).
13. Wording: joins between parts are described honestly as "a short smooth
    fade at each join (no clicks)" rather than "crossfades".
