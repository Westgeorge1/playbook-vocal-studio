PLAYBOOK VOCAL STUDIO 0.26.0 — VOCAL FINISHING WORKBENCH
25 October 2026 • Based on the supplied 0.25.1 application

ADDED
1. Find what needs improving (on POLISH)
   Select 0.1–20 seconds and compare Edited raw, Cleaned, Tuned and Final
   at matched loudness. Each stage has practical advice. Existing uncertain
   notes and large target moves are counted for review, not scored as mistakes.
   No tuning or effect parameter is changed by the comparison.

2. Phrase volume (under the timeline selection on Song & Vocals)
   Add a smooth +/-12 dB move over a chosen word or phrase, with 10–500 ms
   transitions. Edit/remove moves, or use the song Undo/Redo. A gold line shows
   the envelope on each vocal lane. Up to 128 non-overlapping moves per lane.
   The moves are applied AFTER per-lane POLISH, including to a lane using dry
   vocal. They do not invalidate POLISH or change source recordings.
   Song playback, vocal mix, finished mix and polished stems include moves.
   Dry stems exclude them. Saves, reloads and project backups retain them.

3. Finish & check (Song & Vocals, and Export audio)
   Measure the actual combined vocals and optional backing with K-weighted,
   gated loudness and a 4x reconstructed true-peak estimate.
   Keep current loudness, or choose a target; choose a peak ceiling.
   Preserve dynamics adjusts the whole level. The optional gentle limiter
   controls peaks with stereo-linked gain and a verified post-render ceiling.
   Before/after previews are matched in volume. Actual level, maximum limiting
   and any safety trim are shown. Missing a loudness target is reported.
   Download a 24-bit finished WAV and optional JSON measurements.
   This is a separate, explicit finishing render; ordinary exports and song
   playback do not silently acquire a master limiter.

- Backups with phrase-volume moves declare a 0.26.0 playback requirement.
- Corrected old wording that treated -14 LUFS as a universal streaming target.

UNCHANGED
- All seven locked engine files remain byte-identical to 0.25.1.
- No second tuner, automatic extra EQ, or automatic musical decisions added.
- Tuning, source recording, and existing compact-backup precision policies remain.
- No live website was deployed.

VERSION COMPATIBILITY
Use 0.26.0 or newer to preserve the SOUND of phrase-volume moves. Earlier versions
may open the project but ignore the moves and finishing preferences. Keep a
backup before upgrading. Compatibility backups do not teach old apps these new
features. Finished WAVs remain ordinary portable audio files.
