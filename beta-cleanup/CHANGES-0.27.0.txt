PLAYBOOK VOCAL STUDIO 0.27.0 — REFERENCE-GUIDED FINISH

Based on the complete 0.26.0 package.

NEW
- Save one named reference-vocal measurement profile with each song.
- Reuse profiles after reload and project backup/reopen without embedding reference audio.
- Compare voice-like tone, sung-frame variation, high-frequency bursts and stereo width.
- Small, explicit trials using existing EQ, de-esser, word leveling and doubler controls.
- Isolated current/suggested previews, measured loudness matching and peak protection.

- Cancelling or changing the passage invalidates pending/stale previews.
- Entry points in Smart Guide, reference comparison and the POLISH toolbar.

ADOPTION FIXES (our integration, 25 October 2026)
- Fixed: a short Fix phrase or recording could be silently lost. If the
  browser's audio clock had not started yet (busy phone, audio device switching),
  the screen already said "Recording"; pressing Stop then kept nothing.
  Now the Record button says "Stop (mic starting…)" until audio is really
  flowing, and Stop at that moment tells you nothing was recorded so you can
  try again. Once recording, even a 1-second early stop is kept as an attempt.
- New automatic checks: phrase volume and finished-WAV paths (R10), honest
  recording state (R11), and the new quality/reference files are covered by the
  "no own WAV writer" and "no duplicate keys" checks.
- New browser test tests-0.27/mic_start271.py (desktop and phone widths).
- Test runner reads the app version from index.html.
