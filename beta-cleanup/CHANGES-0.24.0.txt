Playbook Vocal Studio 0.24.0 — changes (from the independent review of 0.23.1)

Correctness
- F1  POLISH validity now compares a hash of the lane's real rendered audio, so a fade, trim, gain or timing
      edit always asks for a POLISH update. Old projects are converted on open (unchanged ones keep their edits).
      Validity checks no longer change anything. Undo/redo/save/reopen keep the correct state.
- F2  Listen plays the auditioned take on top inside its range; the rest of the song plays around it. Layer order is unchanged.
- F3  Split is sample-transparent: splitting one recording changes no sample and keeps POLISH valid.
      Where two different takes meet, they crossfade (no dip), and outer fades are kept.
- F4  One song key. Changing it (Song screen or POLISH) marks every polished lane in another key
      "Key update needed", keeps notes and effects, and blocks processed export until updated.
      Per-track override: Track settings › "Keep this vocal in its own key".
- F5  Key, scale, count-in, stop at section end, click volume, backing preference and the mixer are saved through
      the durable journal straight away. The journal is tagged with the song and revision, and never overwrites a newer project.
      Also fixed: a 0.24 development regression that skipped autosave restore.
- F6  Removing the reference take (Delete unchosen, Redo last take, Remove attempt) clears the reference; the confirm says so; Undo restores both.
- F7  Reference melody and timing comparison works when note counts differ (alignment over steady notes, new ref-align.js;
      engine files unchanged). Takes that still can't be compared are labelled in Why suggested.
- F8  Saving for older apps keeps the comp you hear (whole take + phrase fixes, overlaps, splits) and warns exactly where.

Capacity
- Vocal source limit 256 MB = about 23 min 18 s of mono 48 kHz recording (was 128 MB, about 11 min 39 s).
- "Room: … of recording" is shown next to the transport before you record.
- New recordings are stored on the 16-bit grid (−96 dB, bit-exact after save). The autosave writes each recording once
  to its own storage entry at half size and removes unused ones. Song recordings in project ZIPs are 16-bit WAV.
- A main vocal longer than 3 minutes in stereo is opened in mono for the POLISH engine (the engine runs out of memory otherwise).

Phone / UX
- Take editor: Apply / Cancel / Reset stay in a sticky footer; Split and Reference sit in a secondary group.
- Touch targets are at least 44 px on phones and touch screens. Transport stands out; section tools and zoom are quieter.
- "Chosen" explains when a take is used only outside a correction on top.
- POLISH shows "Preview: this vocal" / "Preview: whole song (all lanes)"; the song button is "▶ Whole song".
