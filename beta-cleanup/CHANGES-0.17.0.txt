Playbook Vocal Studio 0.17.0 — changes from 0.16.1
====================================================

What you asked for: "Remove them completely but keep what's necessary, but fuse the part you keep with the new
recording functions." All recording now lives on 1 RECORD & COMP. 2 POLISH only polishes the comp.

------------------------------

    (keys 1–5). An old saved tab "phrase" or "rec" opens CLEAN.
  - Take lanes and phrase lanes on the POLISH timeline, the "Choose" buttons in the lane heads, the record-arm (●) button,
    the IN/OUT punch strip and red flags, the resume line, phrase selection / dragging / right-click menus on the
    POLISH timeline. The POLISH ruler strip now shows your parts as read-only names.
  - Transport: ● REC, Next phrase, CLICK, COUNT, MIC meter, mic-check button, CLIP light, recording status, REC MODE
    button. Only the OUT meter stays. LOOP stays as a listening loop (your dragged ruler region, else the part under
    the playhead, else 4 bars).
  - Menus: Edit › Redo this phrase / Next phrase / Rename phrase / Delete unchosen takes in all phrases / Add marker;
    View › Phrase inspector / Recording setup / big REC MODE / Mic check / Calibrate latency;
    File › Download last microphone file (moved to RECORD & COMP Settings ▾).
  - Keys in POLISH: R (record), Shift+R, M (marker), , and . (marker jumps), C (click), K (count-in), I (mic check).
    R in POLISH now shows a hint "Recording is on step 1".
  - WAVE EDIT: "Record a whole-song take" (mic) button and its timer/meter, and the "record a take with my phone's
    recorder" file input. "Add a whole-song take (audio file)" stays.
  - Session window (File › Session & tracks): "Record" / whole-vocal record button and its notes. It now has a
    button "Record your vocal on 1 RECORD & COMP".

  - Phone REC MODE (the separate big-button screen) and its buttons.
  - Duplicated record buttons: POLISH ● REC, REC MODE ● button, PHRASE-tab Record, REC-tab Redo/Next, WAVE EDIT record,

RECORD & COMP
  - The "More ▾" menu's recording tools (Loop, Punch in now, Metronome, Hear myself, Mic check, Mic gain, Phone REC

    "All recording settings (POLISH › REC tab)"). The menu is now "Settings ▾" and holds only settings and files.

FUSED INTO 1 RECORD & COMP (where each one lives now)
------------------------------------------------------
  Punch-in on the fly ........ R, REC or the big button while the song plays (Play / Space). Recording starts at the
                               playhead in the part under it (a new "Punch m:ss" part is made if there is none).
                               R / REC / big button again = punch out; the song keeps playing and the comp player
                               restarts at once so you hear the new take. Space = punch out and stop, Esc = cancel.
                               The big button says "Punch in here" / "Punch out", REC says PUNCH / OUT.
                               The punched take = your chosen take outside the punch + the new singing inside it,
                               15 ms fades, auto-chosen when the part had a chosen take; one Undo goes back.
  Loop recording ............. "⟲ Loop: ON/OFF" button on the part panel (key L). With Loop ON, Record plays the
                               selected part round and round after the pre-roll / count-in; every full pass is a
                               take. Refused with a plain warning if the part runs past the end of the backing.
  Latency test + calibration . Settings ▾ › Latency … ms, Latency test (timing)…, Use the automatic latency.
                               The value is used for every take: normal, punch-in, loop.
  Metronome .................. CLICK button next to COUNT-IN in the transport (phone: next to the count-in select);
                               key C. Settings ▾ › Click level.
  Count-in ................... COUNT-IN select (transport; phone: "Count-in" select). Also Settings ▾ › Count-in.
  Monitoring ................. HEAR ME button in the transport (phone: HEAR ME). Turning it on shows a headphones
                               warning. Settings ▾ › Hear-me level.
  Mic gain ................... GAIN slider next to the mic meter, with the dB value (phone: GAIN row).
  Mic meter + CLIP ........... MIC LEVEL meter (click it, or key I, for a mic check) + CLIP light (click = reset).
  Mic check messages ......... mic check window before the first take (Settings ▾ › Mic check before the first take)
                               and plain-English microphone errors (blocked / not found / busy).
  Import a take .............. "⤓ Import take…" on the part panel: lines up with the part start, or with the song
                               start (whole-song file; only this part's piece is kept). Too-short files are flagged.
  Backing on/off ............. BACKING ON/OFF.   Hear my comp ....... HEAR MY COMP ON/OFF.
  Undo / Redo ................ ↶ ↷ in the top bar, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z.
  Autosave + backup reminder . saved light in the top bar; Settings ▾ › Backup reminder.
  Rename / resize parts ...... Rename part, drag the part edges (phone: red IN / OUT flags), double-click a part.
  Delete unchosen ............ "Delete unchosen takes (n)" + new "…in all parts (n)" link under the take list.
  Un-choose a take ........... click "✓ Chosen" again (or right-click › Un-choose this take).
  Melody / timing reference .. right-click a take › Use as melody / timing reference.
  Song key for suggestions ... Settings ▾ › Song key (for take suggestions).
  Pre-roll, join fade, tempo . Settings ▾.
  Export comp ................ Export comp (WAV) (top bar; phone: Export comp).
  Comp done → POLISH ......... bottom right (phone: Go to POLISH ▶). In POLISH a "Load my new comp" button appears
                               when the comp changed on RECORD & COMP, and "◀ Record & comp" goes back.
  Backing-end warning ........ status line + toast when a part runs past the end of the backing.
  Unique take names .......... Take 1, Take 2 … per part, never reused (also for punch, loop and import takes).
  Phone ...................... the big record button stays; all the fused controls are on the phone screen.

FIXES
-----
  - Hear-me monitoring stayed on after a normal take ended (your mic kept playing in the headphones). It now switches
    off and the microphone is released when the take ends.
  - Status bar wording "attempts in RECORD" → "takes on RECORD & COMP".

UNDER THE HOOD (honest notes)
-----------------------------
  - The 0.13 core still reads some fields of the old PHRASE and REC panels (part name/start/end boxes, record button,
    status line, count-in/latency/monitor settings). Those elements are kept inside one hidden, inert container
    (#engineRoom) so nothing can see or click them; the screen controls drive them. Deleting them would mean
    rewriting the core's recording code, which also does the audio work, so they stay hidden instead.
  - Audio engine files are byte-identical to 0.16.1 (sha256 in ENGINE-SHA256.txt): phrase-core.js, worker.js,
    phrase-worker.js, engine-wasm.js, engine-wasm.wasm, rec-worklet.js; daw.css and rec.css are unchanged too.
  - Project format unchanged (format 9, workspace v1). New optional take fields: punch {from,to,over} and source
    (imported file name). 0.16.1 opens 0.17 projects (tested); 0.13–0.16 projects open in 0.17 (0.15 and 0.16 tested).
  - Files changed: index.html, rc.js, rc.css, rec.js, daw.js, START-HERE.txt, RECORD-AND-TAKES.txt.
    Patch scripts that made the changes are in tests-0.17/patch_*.py.
