Playbook Vocal Studio 0.17.2 — fixes from the independent review of 0.17.1
(app version 0.17.2 · audio engine version 0.12.1, byte-identical; see REVIEW-RESPONSE-0.17.2.md for finding → fix → test)

Your recording is never thrown away
  - F1 Long punch-ins are kept whole. 0.17.1 cut every punch at 60 s and dropped the rest. Now the part grows to the
    60-s project limit and the rest of the punch goes into "(cont.)" parts (as many as needed), sample-exact.
    One Undo removes the punch and the continuation parts together. The 10-minute safety limit (with a warning one
    minute before) now also applies to punch-ins and loop recording, with a message saying why it stopped.
  - F2 A take that runs past OUT always keeps the whole recording (pre-roll to Stop, silence at the end too). 0.17.1
    trimmed the take at the next part without asking (when the next part was too short to shorten) and cut off the
    silent tail. Now, whenever the singing runs into other parts, a window asks:
        Keep <next part> as it is · Make room / Replace … · Keep it as a take only
    "Replace" names the parts and how many takes it removes; one Undo brings everything back. Short overlaps,
    most of / all of the next part, several following parts and continuation parts that reach another part are
    all handled the same way. Nothing is changed if something fails (the parts are put back exactly).
    A recording that cannot be added is kept for Settings ▾ › Download my last microphone recording (now a WAV
    file for punch-ins), and the recovery copy of a normal take is only cleared after the take is stored.
  - F5 Mic unplugged / lost while recording: the audio so far is kept — a normal take or punch as
    "Take N (mic lost)", a loop pass in progress as "(mic lost, incomplete)" (finished passes are normal takes),
    with a message. Esc (your own cancel) still throws the take away. If the browser cannot read the recording,
    the message points to the recovery download.

POLISH edits are safe
  - F6 Saving a project no longer re-sends the comp to POLISH (that reset note, breath and timing edits). It saves the
    POLISH session as it is and the whole RECORD & COMP workspace. A cancelled download changes nothing.
    Sending a changed comp is always your own action (Comp done / Load latest comp / Export comp) and asks first,
    saying that POLISH edits start again on the new vocal.
    Adding, renaming or deleting takes that are not chosen no longer marks the comp as changed (the app compares
    what the comp is made of: chosen takes, their place, level and timing, the join fade, the starting vocal).
    Projects from older versions fall back to the old rule until the comp is next sent.

Live recording controls
  - F3 GAIN works at once during a normal take too (it was fixed at the start, and skipped at 0 dB). The recording,
    the meter and HEAR ME go through one gain stage that the app owns, also at 0 dB.
  - F4 HEAR ME can be switched on and off at any time: before recording (takes effect when you record), in the mic
    check, during a normal take and during a punch / loop. It uses the same microphone input (never a second
    microphone stream), is released when recording stops, and shows a message if it cannot connect.

Tests (F7)
  - A suite with any failing check (or a crash) now exits with code 1; run_all.sh collects every suite's exit code,
    prints a summary and exits 1 if anything failed (selftest_run_all.sh proves it). Paths, ports and the browser
    can be set with PVS_APP_DIR, PVS_TESTS, PVS_CHROME, URL, URL15/16/170/171, PVS_PYTHON, PVS_OLD_DIR.
  - The POLISH Undo "NOTE" is now a real check (a changed sound setting is put back exactly).
  - New t172.py with the regressions the review asked for (the review's own scripts were not supplied).

Smaller things from the review
  - App version and audio engine version are labelled separately (help › About, Settings ▾ › ABOUT).
  - START-HERE no longer says the hosted link is 0.13.0.
  - Settings ▾ separates "Autosave (this browser only)", "Back up project (.zip with every take)" and
    "Export comp audio (WAV)". The result of a backup (saved / cancelled / failed) appears on the RECORD & COMP status
    line and as a message, also on a phone.
  - "Why suggested?" under each take: pitch, timing and noise numbers, clipping and score — or why there is none
    (analysis failed, pitch uncertain, take does not cover the part, too close to call, not enough takes).

Not changed
  - Audio engine files byte-identical (phrase-core.js, worker.js, phrase-worker.js, engine-wasm.js/.wasm,
    rec-worklet.js; daw.css and rec.css too) — sha256 in ENGINE-SHA256.txt.
  - Every feature of 0.17.1 is still there. Projects from 0.15, 0.16 and 0.17 open; 0.17.2 projects open in 0.17.1
    (and 0.16.1). New project fields (appliedSig) are ignored by older versions.

Files changed: index.html, rec.js, rc.js, rc.css, daw.js, START-HERE.txt, RECORD-AND-TAKES.txt, tests-0.17/*.
