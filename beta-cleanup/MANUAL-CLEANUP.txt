Playbook Vocal Studio 0.32.2 · Manual cleanup · Separate sound
Run from the extracted folder with python -m http.server 8000. Open http://localhost:8000.
In Song & Vocals, record or import a take. Open Edit and choose Manual cleanup · Separate sound.
Drag across a breath, or try Find next possible breath and verify by ear.
Start at 6 dB reduction and 10 ms edge fade. Listen with the words before and after.
For a gust underneath a word try Rumble under voice, 0–100 Hz, 4 dB reduction.
Inspect selected sound shows a spectrogram. Drag a rectangle to set time and frequency.
Use Original selection, My edit and Hear removed to check the result at the same monitor gain.
Loop cleanup preview repeats the region. Stop before selecting another region.
Keep as new take creates an editable copy and stores the settings with the take.
Export edited selection saves an individual note/phrase. Export removed sound saves the difference.
Local spectral edits handle selections up to 30 seconds. They cannot isolate two sources sharing the same time and frequency perfectly.
For whole-track cleanup use POLISH > CLEAN, learn a quiet room sample and audition removed noise.
Pitch correction and approved Snap presets live in the existing note editor. Use the existing EQ, dynamics and Finish & check for final polish.
Autosave keeps the original recording once and saves only the edited stretch of each cleanup take.
