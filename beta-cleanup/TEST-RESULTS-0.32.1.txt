APPROVED SOUND
At the same reference (A = 443.8 Hz, which 0.31.13 chose for the test vocal), no preset, Snap all +
Smooth 25% and 85% correction + Smooth 25% give byte-identical audio to 0.31.13. The Natural engine
with default controls is bit-identical to 0.31.13 over 800 random runs. New vocals now start at the
backing's tuning or A = 440 instead of the singer's centre, by design.

LIMITS
Synthetic vocals are not your voice. Physical phone, real microphone and headphone listening still
need a device check. The AI octave check needs .mjs served as JavaScript and .wasm as application/wasm.
