PLAYBOOK VOCAL STUDIO 0.28.0 — CONSERVATIVE FINISHING
26 October 2026

Purpose
Reduce unnecessary automatic cleaning, make uncertain tuning decisions visible,
and provide a more conservative final export starting point. This update does
not certify professional sound quality or approve one preset for every singer.

CLEANING
- Newly opened raw vocals use Gentle spectral with a 6 dB reduction setting.
  Cleaning starts bypassed when the engine's original-input SNR estimate is
  38 dB or higher, or no finite estimate exists. Otherwise cleaning starts on.
  The 38 dB boundary matches the existing UI's 'Clean' category; this is a
  conservative heuristic, not a reliable classifier of studio-quality audio.
- Saved POLISH settings are restored as saved; they are not silently migrated.
- Try gentle cleaning applies 6 dB, sensitivity 100%, rumble Off, automatic hum,
  and enables Gentle spectral, with Undo. Users may bypass it or adjust further.
- Switching cleaner no longer silently raises reduction to its engine-suggested
  amount. Double-tap reduction returns to 6 dB; rumble returns to Off.
- Clean & Natural now uses the conservative cleaning choice, keeps existing
  breath edits, and does not add automatic 8 dB breath cuts. It still turns off
  tuning and tonal/space processing, and enables the chain limiter at -1.6 dB.
  It is a starting point for audition, not a final approved sound preset.
- Voice Focus and hum guidance no longer promise that sung tone is protected or
  that a hum estimate is conclusive. Compare Before, Cleaned and Removed noise.

PITCH REVIEW
- A visible count in POLISH opens the existing uncertain-note editor.
- Each saved lane stores informational pitchReview metadata from actual engine
  uncertainty flags, separate from the existing 13-value editable-note format.
- Finish & check lists uncertainty for audible processed lanes, respecting Mute,
  Solo, dry bypass and whether automatic tuning is enabled. Review opens that
  lane's existing editor. Nothing forces correction or blocks intentional export.
- Older sessions with no review metadata say that review is not yet checked;
  opening POLISH refreshes it. 'No uncertain notes' is not proof of correct melody.
- The ordinary Export menu also shows a text review summary.

FINAL EXPORT
- An unconfigured Finish & check defaults to -1.6 dBTP. Explicit saved ceilings
  are retained. The pure finisher's unspecified ceiling has the same default.
- Preserve dynamics remains the default. Gentle limiting is still optional.
- Export guidance and measurement JSON explicitly describe the meter as an
  estimate and distinguish it from independent verification of the downloaded WAV.
- The limiter algorithm and 4x meter are unchanged. The new margin was checked
  against the audit's abrupt 12 kHz boundary signal with FFmpeg; it is not a
  guarantee of certified true-peak compliance for every possible signal.

INTEGRATION
New runtime file: finish-policy.js. Include it before index.html's main script.
Modified: index.html, song-polish.js, song-studio.js, quality-studio.js,
quality-core.js (default ceiling only), quality-worker.js (cache version only),
and quality-studio.css (touch-sized review button and phone select layout).
All seven files in ENGINE-SHA256.txt remain byte-identical.
Applying the new raw-vocal starting choice requires an additional engine update
on first opening; this can add processing time, especially on long vocals.
Existing recording, take selection, effects, reference guide, project backup,
and Undo routes are retained. No live site was deployed.

Next step
Listen to your real vocals at matched volume, solo and with the backing. Keep
only processing that improves clarity while retaining the desired warmth,
consonants, vibrato and slides. Numerical checks cannot choose the musical finish.
