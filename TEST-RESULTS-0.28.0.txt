measured -1.601 dBTP in the app and -1.1 dBTP in FFmpeg ebur128. This reproduces
the tested headroom workaround. It does not fix or certify the meter algorithm,
or guarantee the same discrepancy on every input. Explicit saved ceilings remain.

LIMITS
Chromium desktop/headless and emulated phone layout; no physical Android/iOS
microphone, low-memory-device or headphone-latency certification. User vocals were
short test excerpts; this update has not established perceptual quality across all
songs or a universal professional preset. The core DSP, tuning/formant algorithms
and true-peak estimator are unchanged. A new raw vocal requires an additional
engine update to apply the conservative start, which can add opening time.
