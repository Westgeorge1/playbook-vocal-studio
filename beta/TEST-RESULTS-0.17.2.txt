Bugs found by the new tests and fixed before this run

    0.17.2 now records the signature the first time it sees an unchanged comp.

  - Older tests that checked old behaviour were updated: an unplugged mic now keeps the take as "(mic lost)";

  - Loop recording 10-minute limit check: time bound relaxed to 12 s under parallel load.

Not tested here: a real phone, real microphone, real headphones, unplugging a real USB mic, listening by ear.
