- POLISH: drags the settings panel, editor panel and track-name column handles. Checks drag amounts, limits,

  View > Reset panel sizes, and that the dock handle hides while the dock is collapsed.
- Tuning window: resizes from all eight edges and corners. Left and top edges keep the opposite side fixed.
  Checks the 640 px minimum, the size remembered after reopening, and that handles hide while maximized.
- Recording window: resizes from its right edge.
- Note editor: checks the tuning settings column splitter and the docked piano-roll grip.
- Song & vocals: checks the name column (lanes and ruler together) and track height. A track can be shorter than
  its controls, which then scroll. Checks that a lane drag never starts a selection or edits the song.
- Dialogs: moves the song dialog and the take editor, and checks they reopen in their usual place.
- Phones: at 390x844 and 844x390, no handles and no horizontal overflow.
- Small window: at 1000x700, an oversized saved width is limited.
- Project safety: no IndexedDB project keys, dirty flag or Undo entries after resizing, and no page errors.
