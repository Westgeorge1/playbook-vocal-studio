# Playbook Vocal Studio 0.36.0 beta

## Hear and retake a line

When you stop recording, the recording controls identify the vocal, the take and whether it is used in the song. **Hear this take** auditions the recorded vocal in the current mix. It starts at the take with your configured lead-in and leaves the editing playhead where it was. Ordinary waveform Play still plays the song from the playhead. **Record another take** records the same range, keeps the earlier performances, and leaves the earlier choice in place. **Use in song** chooses a complete alternate. Interrupted and incomplete attempts have distinct status text.

**Check my mic** opens from the microphone meter. It shows input level and clipping, uses the Song recording gain, and releases the microphone when closed. The timing-calibration shortcut is separate. No take is created during a mic check.

## Guide a listening decision

Choose a concern at the top of **Guide → Guide this vocal**. For a breath, pitch concern, competing harmony, or two takes, the guide looks for a relevant passage. Your explicit selection wins. If only one performance is available, you can hear it without having to compare it with a missing take.

Before Keep, the guide identifies the recording range, affected phrase or whole vocal. Pitch review can go straight to the notes. **Undo this song edit**, **Undo whole-vocal tuning** and **Undo whole-vocal tone** use their respective histories and remove the corresponding guide decision. A later edit invalidates the shortcut instead of undoing the wrong work.

## Export or keep editing

**Finish & check** identifies whether the export will include the backing both before preparing the WAV and next to Download. The initial choice reflects the monitoring setting. You can change it without changing the monitoring mix. A change clears the prepared export until you prepare it again. **Back up editable project** downloads the ZIP that can be reopened with the takes and guide decisions.
