Reading pitch

Selected note

Drag across the timeline strip at the top of the editor to loop a section; tap it to move the play marker. Tap a note to select it. With the Pitch tool, drag it up or down onto a key, or use the − / + box to fine-tune in cents. With the Time tool, drag the middle of a note to move it earlier or later, or drag its edges to make it shorter or longer. With the Split tool, tap a note where it should be cut in two. Double-tap a note to snap it. Tap a piano key to hear that pitch.

Vocal tuning presets

Choose the song key first. Both presets act on every note, turn on Classic Smooth at 25%, and turn Keep blue notes off. Apply once, then correct individual notes. Undo restores the previous settings.

Correct pitch

Off (normal): every note is tuned into the key. On: a note you clearly sang outside the key stays on that note. Only use it if a song sounds wrong with it off.

Corrects only the notes you've selected, including a single note. Select all to apply these amounts to the whole vocal. Pitch center at 100% puts the selected notes right on the key; 60–85% keeps some of your natural pitch. Snap all corrects every note at once. Automatic tuning below controls correction across the vocal. Natural mode leaves your explicit note edits in charge.

Voice

Changes the character of your voice without changing the notes. Around +0.5 brightens, around −0.5 warms. Past ±2 it starts to sound like a different singer.

Turns down breaths and noise in the pauses between phrases. Consonants at the start and end of words are left alone.

Beat grid

Set the tempo or detect it from your riddim, then line up Bar 1. Quantize moves note starts onto the grid; 50–70% tightens timing but keeps your feel.

Double

Harmonies follow your tuned lead a third above or below, kept in your key. The double is a second take with tiny pitch and timing differences, to thicken the lead. They play in Tuned mode and export as separate tracks.

Note detection

Left cuts notes more readily, for fast runs. Right keeps notes together, for slides and vocals with reverb.

Re-detecting clears your note edits. Undo brings them back.

Optional. Stops high notes (around D#4 and above) being read an octave or more too low, which can add a rough undertone when tuned. Turning it on or off re-reads the pitch and clears note edits; apply your preset again afterwards. Compare by ear before using it on a release.
