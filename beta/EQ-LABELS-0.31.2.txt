FIRST EQ
LOW (Body): the existing Body band.
LOW-MID (Mud): the existing Mud band.
HIGH-MID (Presence): the existing Presence band.
HIGH (Air): the existing Air band.
GAIN: the existing Boost / Cut knob for the selected band, measured in dB.

The four band names are visible in a colour legend and the selected-band
readout. The original frequency, gain and width controls are unchanged.
The first EQ's bands remain freely adjustable in frequency; the labels describe
their usual roles, not new fixed frequency limits.

SECOND EQ
LOW GAIN: the existing Body gain (low shelf).
MID GAIN: the existing Mid gain (bell).
HIGH GAIN: the existing High gain (high shelf).
Advanced frequencies now call Body frequency LOW FREQ.

Gain in these EQs means boost (+) or cut (-) of that frequency band. Zero dB
leaves the band unchanged. It is not a newly added microphone/input gain stage.
Overall vocal loudness remains under Level.

The dynamic-EQ descriptions also identify the low-mid, mid and high-mid bands.
Their reduction controls still say maximum cut because they reduce excess
frequencies dynamically. Settings summaries and reference-guide suggestions use
the same clearer labels.
