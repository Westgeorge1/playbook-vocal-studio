THE SIMPLE WORKFLOW

1. Record your lead vocal.
   Load your instrumental if you have one. Select Lead vocal and press Record.
   Sing the song, then press Stop. The recording stays together as one take.
   Need a beat? Turn on ♩ Click (or press C) and pick a Count-in of 1 or 2 bars.
   Tempo and time signature are under Recording settings (or tap the BPM button).
   🎧 HEAR ME lets you hear your own voice while recording. Use headphones.
   Punch in: press Play, then press R where you want to re-sing; R again to stop.
   The song keeps playing and the new bit blends in with short fades.

2. Fix a word or phrase.
   Drag over that spot, or enter its start and end in seconds. Press Fix phrase.
   Record again as many times as needed. Listen, then Choose the attempt you like.
   The rest of your recording stays in place. Repeat with lead-in makes another
   attempt each pass. A partial attempt is kept but cannot replace a longer phrase.
   ⟲ Loop (or L) records the selection round and round without gaps; every pass
   becomes an attempt. Press Stop when you have enough. Esc cancels a recording.

3. Record part by part (optional).
   Add Intro, Verse, Chorus, Bridge or Outro in Song sections. Click a section (or
   Next part ▶) and press ● Record part. Keep singing past the end if you like: the
   part grows to fit. Tick "Stop at part end" if you want it to stop by itself.
   Every take is kept. Rate takes with the stars; "Suggested" and "Why suggested?"
   show which take the check prefers and why. Choose the one you want.
   Redo last take re-sings the newest take; Delete unchosen takes tidies up.
   Edit on a take trims it, changes its level, adds fades or moves its timing.
   Nothing is cut from the recording; Undo puts it back.
   Split here (in Edit) cuts the range in two at the playhead; both halves keep
   every take. Use as reference marks the take the others are compared with.
   Set the song Key and Scale in Recording settings; POLISH TUNE uses the same key.
   With / Without in the transport chooses whether you hear the backing.

4. Add backing vocals.
   Press + Add vocal track. Name it Harmony, Double, Ad-libs, or anything you like.
   Record and fix phrases on that lane. Your lead and other lanes stay separate.
   OLDER VERSIONS: a project backup opened in 0.18.3 or earlier shows only the
   Lead vocal and its parts. Open it in 0.20.0 or newer to get every track.

5. POLISH each voice.
   Press Polish on a vocal lane. Clean, Tune, note edits and effects now belong
   to that vocal only. Your changes are saved automatically on that track.
   Use the Polishing selector to move between lead and harmony. Each keeps its
   own settings. One voice can have light tuning and another stronger tuning;
   one can have reverb while another stays dry.

6. Hear the result together.
   Press Hear song in POLISH. It plays every vocal lane using its saved processing
   and the Song & vocals mixer. Play below the POLISH bar auditions the selected
   vocal. The existing Mix panel fits backing for that selected-vocal audition;
   full-song playback/export uses the song lane levels instead.
   Back in Song & vocals, adjust each lane's volume, pan, Mute and Solo.
   Use dry vocal bypasses a lane's POLISH while keeping its settings.

7. Export or back up.
   Export song in POLISH offers the finished vocal mix, song with backing,
   separate polished stems, or dry stems. Stems all start at 0:00. A track you
   have not polished stays dry. Muted tracks remain in separate stem exports.
   EXPORT THIS VOCAL in the lower POLISH panel exports the selected voice using
   the original detailed export controls.
   Back up project saves all tracks, attempts, sections and editable POLISH
   settings. Check that the ZIP finished downloading. Autosave is local to this
   browser and is not a replacement for keeping a backup.

IF YOU RECORD A NEW PHRASE AFTER POLISHING

Only the affected vocal shows Update needed. Other vocals keep their processing.
Press Polish on the changed track. The app keeps its cleaning, tuning settings
and effects, but asks before resetting manual note, breath and timing edits that
belonged to the previous performance. Back up first if you need that old version.
Until updated, song playback uses the new dry vocal on that lane. Processed export
asks you to update it or explicitly choose Use dry vocal in its track settings.

USEFUL DETAILS

- Use headphones so backing music does not leak acoustically into your microphone.
- R starts/stops recording; Space stops recording or plays/pauses on Song & vocals.
- A selected phrase gives an exact recording range. R during playback starts with
  lead-in; it is not an instantaneous gapless live punch.
- Use project backups before removing recordings. Song Undo lasts this session.
- WAV exports use 32-bit float audio. Effect tails are retained in processed audio.
- Projects with these features require version 0.20.0 or newer. Older apps cannot
  restore all lanes and settings. Keep the original ZIP if trying an older app.
