Playbook Vocal Studio 0.17.0 (1 RECORD & COMP + 2 POLISH)

Open in Chrome (test link, 0.17.0):
https://westgeorge1.github.io/playbook-vocal-studio/beta/
(The main link https://westgeorge1.github.io/playbook-vocal-studio/ is unchanged.)

All recording happens on 1 RECORD & COMP: parts, takes, punch-in, loop recording, import, count-in, click,
mic level and gain, hear-me monitoring. 2 POLISH works on your comp: CLEAN, TUNE, CHAIN, DETAIL, MIX,
note editor, wave edit, compare reference, save / export. Read RECORD-AND-TAKES.txt.

For your own hosting, serve all files together over HTTPS. Opening index.html directly as a local file cannot reliably run workers or microphone capture. The WASM effects engine remains 0.12.1.

All 12 automated suites passed; two source review/debug passes completed. Physical phone microphone, audibility and touch testing remains outstanding; see VALIDATION.txt.

0.14.0: new DAW-style screen (menu bar, transport, timeline with take lanes, inspector, editor dock, keyboard shortcuts; press ? for shortcuts). Every 0.13 control is still there; see CHANGES-0.14.0.txt for where each one moved. 

0.15.0: true punch-in on the fly (R while playing), draggable IN/OUT flags on the ruler, Redo-this-phrase (R) and Next-phrase (Shift+R), multi-step Undo/Redo for takes and phrases, count-in and metronome, mic level check with clip warning, star ratings and Delete unchosen takes, loop recording (a new take every pass), latency calibration, song markers, big-button REC MODE for phones, optional input monitoring, mic gain, autosave indicator and backup reminders. Everything from 0.14 is still there and 0.13/0.14 projects open unchanged. See CHANGES-0.15.0.txt and the new REC tab. The hosted link above is still 0.13.0 until you upload this folder.

0.16.0: the app now opens on 1 RECORD & COMP — a simple screen for recording the song part by part, several takes per part, choosing the best take of each part and joining them into one vocal (the comp). Press "Comp done → Go to POLISH" (or click 2 POLISH at the top) for the full 0.15 studio: Clean, Tune, Chain, Mix. Extras such as loop recording, punch-in, metronome, monitoring, latency test and REC MODE are under More ▾ and still in POLISH. See CHANGES-0.16.0.txt.

0.16.1: bug fixes from a full debug and review of 0.16.0 (laptop lanes, download box over the POLISH button, clearer messages, slow-song count-in fix). Same projects, same sound engine. See CHANGES-0.16.1.txt and REVIEW-0.16.1.md.

0.17.0: the old recording tools in POLISH are gone (PHRASE and REC tabs, take lanes in POLISH, the phone REC MODE, extra record buttons, star ratings, song markers). The useful ones are now ordinary buttons on 1 RECORD & COMP: punch-in on the fly (R or REC while the song plays, R again to punch out — the song keeps playing), a Loop switch on the part (every pass = a take), CLICK next to the count-in, HEAR ME (monitoring, with a headphones warning), a GAIN slider next to the mic meter and Import take. Settings ▾ now holds only settings and files (latency test, pre-roll, tempo, key, backup reminder, open/save). Same project format as 0.13–0.16; old projects open, 0.17 projects open in 0.16. See CHANGES-0.17.0.txt, TEST-RESULTS-0.17.0.txt and REVIEW-0.17.0.md.
