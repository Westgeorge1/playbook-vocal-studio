Playbook Vocal Studio 0.18.3 — fixes from the independent review of 0.18.2
App version 0.18.3 · audio engine version 0.12.1 (engine files byte-identical, see ENGINE-SHA256.txt)

Everything in 0.18.2 is kept: all features, the tidy RECORD & COMP screen and the project format.
0.18.2 projects open in 0.18.3 and 0.18.3 projects open in 0.18.2 (tested both ways).

Fixes
  01 Import vocal › "Back up first, then import" now goes on to the file picker ONLY after the backup really
     downloaded. If the backup cannot be prepared, the download fails or you cancel it, nothing is imported,
     your parts and takes stay, and a window says so with "Try the backup again" or "Keep my takes".
     (Inside: saveProject() returns {ok, status: saved / cancelled / failed / blocked, msg}.)
  02 Settings ▾ › Back up project works in every session that has something to keep: a backing only,
     Recovered audio only, a whole vocal, or parts and takes. One rule decides this for the menu, Ctrl+S and
     the save itself (canSaveProject). When it is not available the menu item says why.
  03 Tempo (and the other song settings: guide, tempo sync) now come back after a reload when you only have
     parts, takes not yet sent to POLISH, or only a backing. 75 → 100 BPM, reload → 100 BPM.
  04 Song length counts an imported whole vocal (at its own sample rate): a 36 s vocal with no backing and no
     parts gives a 36 s song; Song fit, scrolling, the waveform and the clock all use it; also 2:30 at 44.1 kHz.
  05 The backing level is one setting: the lane fader, the BACKING ▾ menu level, the mixer faders and the
     labels all move together and the level is saved at once (reload and the project keep it; playback uses it).
  06 Trim and split re-run the take check ("suggested", the numbers) of every take they affect — both sides of
     a split — and so do Undo and Redo. An old result can no longer stay on a changed part.
  07 At 900 and 1024 px wide (and at 125 % / 150 % browser zoom) nothing is cut off any more: below 1366 px
     the transport gets compact and wraps onto a second row, the part panel narrows, and the screen is never
     wider than the window. Record, Stop, Settings ▾, the big Record button and Comp done stay reachable.
     From 1366 px the transport stays on one row as in 0.18.2 (the COMP lane stays in view at 1366 × 768).
  08 The question windows (Import a whole vocal?, overlaps, clear, …) are real dialogs: Tab and Shift+Tab stay
     inside, the page behind cannot be used, the title names the dialog for screen readers, Esc picks the safe
     first choice, Enter the focused one, focus goes back to the button you came from, and page shortcuts
     (R, Space, Ctrl+Z…) do nothing while a question is open. A question over the take editor works the same.

Easier to use (still tidy)
  - An empty session shows a START card (laptop: under the lanes; phone: in the part panel): Record a vocal / Import a vocal / Open a project,
    and + Add backing.
  - "What each area does / The flow" is folded behind "Help" once you have a part; the footer text
    "How it works" is behind a small Help link. Open it whenever you like; once you open or close it yourself, it stays that way.
  - The COMP lane says what it is: "your chosen takes joined into one vocal".
  - The backing level is labelled as a LISTENING level (what you hear; not recorded and not the export mix);
    Hear-me and Click levels are labelled as listening levels too.
  - Buttons that cannot be used right now say why: in the tooltip (" — Not available now: …"), and under the
    big Record button.
  - RECORD-AND-TAKES.txt and the old "Apply" / "More ▾" wording now match the lane menus and the
    Comp done / Load my new comp flow.

Tests
  - tests-0.18/t183.py: regression checks for all 8 findings through the real buttons and menus, plus the
    start card / help, 900 / 1024 / 1280 / 1366 px and 125 % / 150 % zoom, phones 360 / 390, failed and
    cancelled backups, and 0.18.2 ↔ 0.18.3 projects. The same checks fail on 0.18.2.
  - run_all.sh also runs t182.py at 1024 × 768. polish17.py now runs right after sessA.py (it opens the project
    sessA makes; before, it could pick up an old copy).
  - Every test input ships in the zip (imp-song.wav and the 0.15 / 0.16 project zips were missing in 0.18.2);
    vocal36.wav and vocal150-44k.wav are made the same way every time by tests-0.18/mkfix183.py.
