Help: what each area does and the flow

WHAT EACH AREA DOES

  1. Two-step switch: record & comp first, then POLISH. Autosave, Undo/Redo, Export comp.
  2. Transport: play, stop, RECORD, position, count-in + CLICK, mic level + CLIP light + gain, HEAR ME (monitoring), backing / comp on-off. While the song plays, REC (or R) punches in right where you are.
  3. Sections strip: the song in sections. Click one; red IN / OUT flags = where the section starts and ends. Recording keeps going until you press Stop; if you sing past OUT, the section grows to fit (turn “Stop at section end” ON to stop at OUT). Drag a section's edge to make it longer or shorter. Double-click to rename.
  4. Take lanes: every try stacked under its section. Green ✓ = the take you chose; grey = spares. Click a take to hear it, double-click to choose it.
  5. Comp lane: your chosen takes joined into one continuous vocal, with a short smooth fade at each join (no clicks).
  6. Section panel: big record button, Redo, Loop (a new take every pass), Import a take, next / previous section, play-choose-delete each take, progress.

your voice beforeafter the chainEQ curvedrag a point: left/right = frequency, up/down = boost/cut

GAIN boosts (+) or cuts (−) the selected band in dB; 0 dB leaves that band unchanged. Select a coloured point to choose its band. FREQUENCY chooses where it acts; WIDTH controls how much of the surrounding range it affects. Overall vocal loudness is controlled under Level.

TAME BOOM and TAME HARSH are a dynamic EQ: they turn the low-mid boom range (around 250 Hz) or the high-mid harsh range (around 3 kHz) down only on the notes where it builds up. The EQ points above set the steady tone.

Dynamics & tone

PEAK CONTROL is a fast first compressor that only catches sudden loud words, so COMPRESS can stay gentle: two light stages sound smoother than one hard one. WORD LEVELING gently evens out loud and quiet words before both (at most ±6 dB). Try 20–30 %.

Second EQ · Low, Mid & High

Advanced frequencies

Final tone shaping after compression and warmth, before echo and reverb. LOW GAIN (Body) adjusts warmth and bass; MID GAIN adjusts the middle frequencies; HIGH GAIN adjusts brightness and air. Each boosts (+) or cuts (−) its band by up to 6 dB; 0 dB is unchanged. Low and High are shelf filters; Mid is a bell. Double-tap a gain knob to return it to 0 dB.

Echo & space

ECHO DUCK turns the echoes down while you sing and lets them bloom in the gaps (up to 18 dB), so your words stay clear. The reggae and dub way.

Shortcuts are ignored while you type in a box. POLISH timeline: click = move the playhead, drag the ruler = loop region. Recording, phrase attempts and song sections live on 1 SONG & VOCALS.




1 · Record your song. In SONG & VOCALS, select Lead vocal, load backing music if needed, then press Record. Sing and press Stop. A whole performance stays together.


2 · Fix a word or phrase. Drag over its range, or enter Start and End in seconds. Press Fix phrase. Repeat until comfortable, then Listen and Choose. The rest of the performance stays in place. Repeat with lead-in adds an attempt each pass.


3 · Organise and layer. Song sections label Intro, Verse, Chorus and Outro without cutting recordings. Add vocal track gives harmonies, doubles and ad-libs their own lane.


4 · Polish each voice. Press Polish on a lane. Clean, Tune, note edits and effects belong to that voice. Use the Polishing selector to work on another track; each retains its settings. Hear song plays the processed tracks together.


5 · Save your result. Export song offers the vocal mix, song with backing, polished stems or dry stems. Back up project keeps all tracks, attempts and editable POLISH sessions. Check that the ZIP finished downloading.


After re-recording: only that track needs a POLISH update. Its sound settings are kept, but the app asks before resetting manual edits attached to the old performance. Other vocals stay unchanged.

What the analyzer can tell you
It compares stable note centres, clipping and background contrast when a recorded lead-in is available. Without a reference take, pitch is measured against notes in your selected key—not the intended melody. With a reference and matching note counts, it also estimates melody and timing differences. These are technical estimates, not a measure of emotion, lyrics or artistic quality. Vibrato and expressive slides are not scored as mistakes. Background contrast does not measure all aspects of clarity or room sound.
