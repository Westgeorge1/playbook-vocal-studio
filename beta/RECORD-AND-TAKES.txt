RECORD & COMP — recording your song part by part (Playbook Vocal Studio 0.17.0)

The idea
  Your song is split into PARTS (Intro, Verse 1, Chorus …). Each part gets as many TAKES as you like.
  You CHOOSE the best take of each part (green ✓). The chosen takes are joined into one vocal: the COMP.
  When every part is chosen, press "Comp done → Go to POLISH" to clean, tune and mix the comp.

1. Get ready
  - Settings ▾ › Open backing track (the beat). BACKING ON/OFF hears it or not.
  - Wear headphones. Sing a line and watch MIC LEVEL. If CLIP lights up, move back or lower GAIN
    (the slider next to the meter). Click the meter (or press I) for a mic check without recording.
  - Settings ▾ › Latency test: do it once per device and browser so takes land on the beat.
    The latency is used for every take (normal, punch-in, loop).

2. Add parts
  - "+ Add part": pick a name, a length in bars, and where it starts (after the last part or at the playhead).
  - Drag a part's edges on the timeline (phone: the red IN / OUT flags) to change where it starts or ends.
  - Rename part / Delete part are above the big button. Overlapping parts: the newer part wins in the comp.

3. Record takes
  - Select a part, press the big red button (or R, or REC). The backing starts a little before the part
    (pre-roll; set it in Settings ▾), you can have a COUNT-IN of 1 or 2 bars and a CLICK (metronome, key C).
    Recording stops by itself at the end of the part. Esc throws the take away.
  - "↺ Redo last take" records the part again and replaces the last take (Undo brings it back).
  - "Next part ▶" (or Shift+R to go there and record).
  - HEAR ME: hear your own voice in the headphones while you record. Wired headphones only — with speakers
    it can echo or howl and the speaker sound gets into your take.

4. Punch-in on the fly
  - Press Play (Space). When the song reaches the spot, press R (or REC, or the big button — it now says
    "Punch in here"). Recording starts right there, in the part under the playhead (a new "Punch m:ss" part
    is made if there is none). Press R again to punch out: the song KEEPS PLAYING and you hear the new take.
    Space punches out and stops. Esc cancels.
  - The punched take is a new take of that part: your chosen take outside the punch, the new singing inside
    it, with short fades (silence outside if the part had no chosen take yet). If the part already had a
    chosen take, the punched take is chosen automatically; one Undo puts the old choice back.

5. Loop recording
  - Turn "⟲ Loop: ON" (key L) and press Record. The part plays round and round (after the pre-roll and
    count-in). Every full pass becomes a new take. Press R / REC / Stop to finish; the pass you were in the
    middle of is dropped. A part that runs past the end of the backing cannot loop (you get a warning).

6. Import a take
  - "⤓ Import take…": pick an audio file. "Lines up with the start of this part" puts the file's start at the
    part's start. "With the start of the song (0:00)" is for a whole-song vocal exported from another app:
    only this part's piece is kept. A file shorter than the part is kept but cannot be chosen.

7. Choose and build the comp
  - ▶ plays a take (with the backing if Settings ▾ › Hear backing when I play a take is ON).
  - "✓ Choose" uses the take in the comp; click "✓ Chosen" again to un-choose it. Double-click a take works too.
  - Right-click a take: listen, choose, rename, use as melody / timing reference, delete.
  - "Delete unchosen takes" (this part) and "…in all parts".
  - ▶ Play on the transport plays the comp (HEAR MY COMP ON) with the backing.
  - Export comp (WAV) saves the joined vocal. "Comp done → Go to POLISH" sends it to POLISH.

8. Saving
  - Autosave keeps your session in the browser. The saved light shows it. Backup reminders tell you to save a
    project file now and then (Settings ▾ › Backup reminder).
  - Settings ▾ › Save project with every take (Ctrl+S) makes a ZIP with every take. Open it with
    Settings ▾ › Open song project. Projects from 0.13–0.16 open; 0.17 projects open in 0.16 too.

Keys on RECORD & COMP
  R record / punch in / punch out   Shift+R next part and record   Space play / pause (stop while recording)
  Home or Enter back to the start   N / P next / previous part      L Loop on/off   C click on/off   I mic check
  Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo / redo   Ctrl+S save project   Esc cancel the recording   2 go to POLISH

Limits
  32 takes per part, 128 takes per song, 30 minutes of take audio, parts 0.5–60 s, song up to 15 minutes.
