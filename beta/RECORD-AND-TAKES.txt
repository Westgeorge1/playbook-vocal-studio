RECORD & COMP — recording your song part by part (Playbook Vocal Studio 0.18.3)

The idea
  Your song is split into PARTS (Intro, Verse 1, Chorus …). Each part gets as many TAKES as you like.
  You CHOOSE the best take of each part (green ✓). The chosen takes are joined into one vocal: the COMP.
  When every part is chosen, press "Comp done → Go to POLISH" to clean, tune and mix the comp.
  An empty session shows a START card: Record a vocal / Import a vocal / Open a project, and + Add backing.
  Once you have a part, the long help is folded away; the "Help" link at the bottom opens it again.

1. Get ready
  - Load the backing (the beat): + Add backing on the start card, or BACKING ▾ on the backing lane
    (Load / Replace / Remove backing, Undo remove, Listening level). BACKING With / Without in the transport
    (or M on the lane) decides whether you hear it while you record. The backing level is a LISTENING level:
    what you hear in your headphones. It is not recorded and it is not the export mix (that is POLISH › Mix).
  - Wear headphones. Sing a line and watch MIC LEVEL. If CLIP lights up, move back or lower GAIN
    (the slider next to the meter). Click the meter (or press I) for a mic check without recording.
  - Settings ▾ › Latency test: do it once per device and browser so takes land on the beat.
    The latency is used for every take (normal, punch-in, loop).

2. Add parts
  - "+ Add part": pick a name, a length in bars, and where it starts (after the last part or at the playhead).
  - Drag a part's edges on the timeline (phone: the red IN / OUT flags) to change where it starts or ends.
  - Rename part / Delete part are above the big button. Overlapping parts: the newer part wins in the comp.

3. Record takes
  - Select a part, press the big red button (or R, or REC). The backing starts a little before the part
    (pre-roll; set it in Settings ▾), you can have a COUNT-IN of 1 or 2 bars and a CLICK (metronome, key C).
    Sing, then press Stop yourself (R, Space, the big button or ■). Recording does NOT stop at the end of the
    part: the backing keeps playing and you keep recording, even after the backing ends. Esc throws the take away.
  - Your recording is never thrown away. The take always keeps everything from the pre-roll to the moment you
    pressed Stop (silence at the end too). The part's IN and OUT only decide which piece the comp uses.
  - Sang past the part's OUT flag? The part gets longer to fit (up to the end of your singing; silence after
    your last note does not count). One Undo puts it back. If that would run into other parts, a window asks:
      Keep <next part> as it is  = this part ends where the next one starts; the rest stays in the take, unused;
      Make room / Replace …      = this part grows; the next part starts later, or — if your singing covers it
                                   (almost) completely — it is replaced (the window says how many takes that
                                   removes; one Undo brings everything back);
      Keep it as a take only     = no part changes at all; the take still holds the whole recording.
    A part can be at most 60 seconds long; anything longer goes into new "(cont.)" parts right after it.
  - "⏹ Stop at part end: OFF/ON" (part panel, saved in your settings): ON brings back the old way — recording
    stops by itself at the part's OUT flag, and a punch-in punches out there.
  - One take can be at most 10 minutes (a safety limit; you get a warning 1 minute before). Whenever recording
    stops without you pressing Stop, a message says why (part end with the switch ON, safety limit, mic
    unplugged, app sent to the background, audio paused by the phone). The same 10-minute limit applies to
    punch-ins and loop recording.
  - Mic unplugged or lost while recording (normal take, punch-in or loop)? What was recorded up to then is KEPT
    as a take marked "(mic lost)" — a loop pass in progress as "(mic lost, incomplete)". Only Esc (your own
    cancel) throws a take away. If a recording ever cannot be added, it is kept as Recovered audio: a
    "⚠ Recovered audio" badge appears in the top bar; open it to download or remove each recording.
  - "↺ Redo last take" records the part again and replaces the last take (Undo brings it back).
  - "Next part ▶" (or Shift+R to go there and record).
  - HEAR ME: hear your own voice in the headphones while you record. You can turn it on or off at any time,
    also in the middle of a take or a punch (it uses the same microphone input, never a second one). If it
    cannot connect, a message says so. Wired headphones only — with speakers it can echo or howl.
  - GAIN works at once, also while you record: the recording, the meter and HEAR ME all go through it.

4. Punch-in on the fly
  - Press Play (Space). When the song reaches the spot, press R (or REC, or the big button — it now says
    "Punch in here"). Recording starts right there, in the part under the playhead (a new "Punch m:ss" part
    is made if there is none). Press R again to punch out: the song KEEPS PLAYING and you hear the new take.
    Space punches out and stops. Esc cancels. A punch keeps going past the part's OUT flag (the part grows,
    as above, with the same choices if it runs into other parts) and even past the end of the song, until you
    punch out. A punch longer than 60 s is kept whole: it continues in "(cont.)" parts.
  - The punched take is a new take of that part: your chosen take outside the punch, the new singing inside
    it, with short fades (silence outside if the part had no chosen take yet). If the part already had a
    chosen take, the punched take is chosen automatically; one Undo puts the old choice back.

5. Loop recording
  - Turn "⟲ Loop: ON" (key L) and press Record. The part plays round and round (after the pre-roll and
    count-in). Every full pass becomes a new take. Press R / REC / Stop to finish; the pass you were in the
    middle of is dropped. A part that runs past the end of the backing cannot loop (you get a warning).

6. Import a take, or a whole vocal
  - VOCAL ▾ on the COMP lane › Import vocal (whole song): one finished vocal file for the whole song. If you
    already have takes you are asked first. "Back up first, then import" downloads a project backup and only
    then asks for the vocal file. If the backup fails or you cancel it, nothing is imported and your parts and
    takes stay as they are; choose "Try the backup again" or "Keep my takes".
  - "⤓ Import take…": pick an audio file. "Lines up with the start of this part" puts the file's start at the
    part's start. "With the start of the song (0:00)" is for a whole-song vocal exported from another app:
    only this part's piece is kept. A file shorter than the part is kept but cannot be chosen.

7. Choose and build the comp
  - ▶ plays a take (with the backing if Settings ▾ › Hear backing when I play a take is ON).
  - "suggested" on a take is a hint from the app. Tap "Why suggested?" (or "Why not suggested?") under the take
    name to see the numbers: pitch (cents off the key or the melody reference), timing (ms against the
    reference take), noise (voice above the background, dB), clipping, the score — or why there is no score
    (analysis failed, pitch uncertain, take does not cover the part, too close to call).
  - "✓ Choose" uses the take in the comp; click "✓ Chosen" again to un-choose it. Double-click a take works too.
  - Right-click a take: listen, choose, rename, use as melody / timing reference, delete.
  - Edit on a take opens the take editor: level, silence, fade in / out make a NEW take (the original stays);
    trim, split and move change the part. The take check ("suggested", the numbers) runs again after trim,
    split, Undo and Redo.
  - "Delete unchosen takes" (this part) and "…in all parts".
  - ▶ Play on the transport plays the comp (HEAR MY COMP ON) with the backing. The COMP lane is your chosen
    takes joined into one vocal; VOCAL ▾ › Play vocal plays it without the backing.
  - Export comp audio (WAV) saves the joined vocal as a sound file. "Comp done → Go to POLISH" sends it to POLISH.
  - Changed your comp after POLISH edits? POLISH keeps the vocal it has until you send the new comp yourself
    (Comp done, or "Load my new comp" in POLISH). You are asked first, because note, breath and timing edits
    made in POLISH start again on the new vocal. Adding, renaming or deleting takes you did not choose does not
    change the comp.

8. Saving — three different things
  - AUTOSAVE (this browser only): automatic after every change; the saved light shows it. It is not a backup —
    a phone can clear browser storage.
  - BACK UP PROJECT: Settings ▾ › Back up project (.zip with every take) (Ctrl+S). A file you keep, with every
    take, your choices and your POLISH edits exactly as they are (saving never re-sends the comp or resets
    edits). It works in every session that has something to keep: a backing only, Recovered audio only, a whole
    vocal, or parts and takes. When it is not available the menu says why. The result — saved, cancelled or
    failed — is shown on the status line and in a message, also on a phone. Open it with Settings ▾ › Open
    song project. Projects from 0.13–0.18 open; tempo, key and backing level come back with them. Backup reminders tell you to do this now and then (Settings ▾ › Backup reminder).
  - EXPORT COMP AUDIO (WAV): only the joined vocal as a sound file, for other apps. Not a project backup.

Keys on RECORD & COMP
  R record / punch in / punch out   Shift+R next part and record   Space play / pause (stop while recording)
  Home or Enter back to the start   N / P next / previous part      L Loop on/off   C click on/off   I mic check
  Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo / redo   Ctrl+S save project   Esc cancel the recording   2 go to POLISH

Limits
  Buttons that cannot be used right now say why in their tooltip (and under the big Record button).
  32 takes per part, 128 takes per song, 30 minutes of take audio, parts 0.5–60 s, song up to 15 minutes.
