PLAYBOOK VOCAL STUDIO — RECORD & TAKES (phone 0.15.0)

Open the browser app directly in Chrome. Wear headphones.

1. Open RECORD, then Load backing track. OPEN also has a backing-only preview
   and its volume/mute controls. The microphone records only your voice; the
   backing plays in your headphones. Speaker playback may bleed into the mic.
2. Tap Continue from here to create your first phrase. Change Start and End
   in seconds, give the phrase a name and tap Update this phrase's boundaries.
   The default is eight seconds. A phrase can be 0.5–60 seconds long.
3. Set a lead-in, then Record this phrase. Watch the countdown and mic meter.
   You hear the backing and existing vocal before the phrase. The old vocal is
   muted inside the phrase. Recording stops just after End. Stop and keep
   attempt lets you finish early; incomplete attempts remain available.
4. Tap Redo this part for another attempt. No earlier attempt is overwritten.
   Alternatively import a phrase recording; its beginning is placed at Start.
5. Listen to each take with or without backing. Playback levels are matched
   for comparison. Use Alignment if a recording sounds early or late; positive
   milliseconds move it later. Assembly level changes its final contribution.
6. Set the song's key and scale if known. You can choose one attempt as a melody
   and timing reference. Read the estimated pitch, clipping and background
   measurements. The suggestion is advisory: choose the delivery you prefer.
7. Tap Choose. The resume position moves to that phrase's end. Continue from
   here starts the next phrase. You can tap the timeline or use the resume
   slider to move elsewhere. Select any earlier phrase to redo it again.
8. Hear assembled vocal + backing checks the whole sequence. Join fade softens
   the edges (20 ms by default). Later-created phrases replace earlier ones in
   overlaps. Unchosen regions retain the starting vocal or silence.
9. Apply chosen takes to vocal loads the assembly into CLEAN, TUNE and CHAIN.
   Current sound settings remain; note and performance edits on the previous
   assembly reset. The source attempts stay in RECORD. Finish the sound, then
   use SAVE to export the vocal or rough mix.
10. Save project with every take downloads a complete song ZIP, including the
    backing, original attempts, choices and resume position. Reopen it through
    OPEN > Song project. Phone 0.13.0 or newer restores editable phrases;
    desktop opens the assembled vocal. Browser autosave is also available but
    is not a replacement for downloaded backups.

Clear take choice restores the starting vocal (or silence) for that phrase
when you Apply again. Remove deletes an attempt/phrase from the workspace;
Undo last change brings it back. OPEN's Clear vocal & takes removes all vocal
work while keeping the backing. New session clears everything. Undo clear is
available until a new vocal or recording replaces that undo point.

Keep the page visible while recording. Switching away stops and keeps the
attempt. If decoding fails but microphone bytes exist, Download last microphone
file for recovery saves them for another import. A storage warning means you
should download a project immediately. Microphone permissions, sound output and
latency vary by phone and headphones; the alignment control is provided for
listening corrections.

NEW IN 0.15 — FASTER RECORDING
- R = Redo this phrase (another take). Shift+R = Next phrase (starts where the
  selected phrase ends). Both are also buttons in PHRASE, REC and REC MODE.
- Punch-in on the fly: press Play, then R where you want to start singing. The
  beat keeps going. R again punches out (playback continues); Space punches out
  and stops; Esc throws the take away. The take lands on the phrase at that
  spot (it is extended if you sang longer) or makes a new "Punch" phrase.
- Drag the red IN / OUT flags on the ruler to move the phrase start and end.
- Loop recording: turn LOOP on (phrase or dragged ruler region), press Play,
  then R. Every full pass becomes a new take on that phrase.
- CLICK (metronome) and count-in (1/2/4 bars) use the BPM. The click is only in
  your headphones; it is never recorded or exported.
- MIC CHECK shows your level and a CLIP light before you record. MIC GAIN in
  the REC tab is a digital boost/cut; browsers cannot change the phone's real
  mic hardware level.
- REC tab > Latency > Calibrate: once per phone + headphones. Tap along or use
  a loopback test; the value is stored for that device and applied to new takes.
  Bluetooth headphones usually need it most.
- Hear old vocal while recording (on = 0.14 behaviour). Input monitoring (hear
  yourself) is OFF by default: it adds a delay on most phones, so prefer wired
  headphones if you turn it on.
- Rate takes with stars, rename takes/phrases by double-clicking (long-press on
  a phone), Delete unchosen takes to tidy up. Undo/Redo go back many steps.
- Markers (M, + Marker) name song sections; drag them on the ruler.
- The "saved" light in the menu bar shows autosave; you are reminded to Save
  project as a ZIP after several new takes.
- On a phone, REC MODE gives big buttons: Record, Listen, Choose, Next.
