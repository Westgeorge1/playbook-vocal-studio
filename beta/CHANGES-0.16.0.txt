PLAYBOOK VOCAL STUDIO 0.16.0 — RECORD & COMP screen (2026-10-18)
Built from 0.15.0. Nothing was taken out. The sound engine is unchanged.

WHAT IS NEW, IN PLAIN WORDS
The app now opens on a simple screen called "1 RECORD & COMP". It is made
for one job: record your song part by part, sing each part as many times as
you like, pick the best take of each part, and join them into one vocal.
When that is done, press "Comp done → Go to POLISH" and you are in the
0.15 screen you already know (Clean · Tune · Chain · Mix).

THE TWO STEPS (top of the screen)
  1 RECORD & COMP   the new simple screen (opens first every time)
  2 POLISH          the full 0.15 studio, exactly as before
  Also at the top: "saved" light (autosave), Undo, Redo, Export comp (WAV).

RECORD & COMP — WHAT EACH AREA DOES
  Transport   start, play, stop, big REC, bar/beat and time, BPM and key,
              count-in (Off / 1 bar / 2 bars), mic level with CLIP light,
              Backing ON/OFF, Hear my comp ON/OFF, More ▾.
  Parts strip the song cut into named, coloured parts (Intro, Verse 1,
              Chorus, Bridge, Outro...). "+ Add part" adds one (pick a name
              and a length in bars). Drag a part's edge to make it longer
              or shorter (snaps to the beat; hold Shift for free). Double-click
              a part to rename it. The selected part shows red IN / OUT flags
              and the pre-roll shading.
  Take lanes  every take stacked under its part. Green with ✓ = the take
              you chose. Grey = spares. Click a take to hear it, double-click
              to choose it. Right-click for the full take menu (stars,
              rename, remove...). Empty parts show "not recorded".
  COMP lane   your chosen takes joined into one vocal, with the same smooth
              join fade as before (× marks the joins). Click it to play the
              comp from there. Play = the whole comp with the backing.
  Part panel  big "Record take N" button (or press R), Redo last take,
              Previous / Next part, the take list (Play, Choose, Del),
              "Delete unchosen takes" (asks first), comp progress
              ("X of Y parts chosen") and "Still to do".

RECORDING A TAKE
  Pick a part and press Record (or R). The backing starts just before the
  part (pre-roll), the count-in clicks if it is on, recording runs from IN
  to OUT and stops by itself. Your latency setting is applied as before.
  The new take is selected so you can listen straight away.
  Redo last take: records again and replaces the last take; if you change
  your mind, one Undo brings the old take back.
  Undo / Redo work for recording, choosing, deleting, renaming and resizing.

EXPORT AND POLISH
  Export comp (WAV) saves ONE vocal file (24-bit, the whole song from 0:00)
  named "<song> - comp vocal.wav", and loads the same comp into POLISH.
  Comp done → Go to POLISH sends the comp (asks first if some parts have no
  chosen take, or if the comp changed since you last sent it) and opens CLEAN.

EVERYTHING ELSE IS STILL THERE — under More ▾
  Loop recording, Punch in now (while playing), Metronome, Hear myself
  (monitoring), Mic level check, Mic gain, Latency test, Phone REC MODE,
  Star ratings on takes, Add song marker, Import a take from a file,
  Un-choose, Rename / Delete part, Delete unchosen takes in all parts,
  Pre-roll length, Join crossfade, BPM, Backing ON/OFF, Hear my comp,
  Audition takes with the backing, Open backing track, Save / Open project,
  Open the REC tab in POLISH, Keyboard shortcuts.
  All of the 0.15 buttons, tabs, menus and shortcuts still work in POLISH.

KEYS ON RECORD & COMP
  R record a take · Space play / pause · Home or Enter back to start
  N next part · P previous part · L loop recording · 2 go to POLISH
  Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo · Ctrl+S save project · Esc closes menus

PHONE
  Same screen in a phone layout: part chips (swipe), a small view of the part
  with draggable IN / OUT flags, the big round Record button, Play / Stop,
  Redo, Next part, the take list, progress, Export comp and Go to POLISH.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - "Hear my comp" OFF plays only the backing, and also turns off hearing
    your old vocal while you record (the same switch as 0.15 "Hear old vocal").
  - Punch-in that does not cover a whole part makes its own small part
    called "Punch m:ss" (same rule as 0.15).
  - A link ending in ?step=polish opens straight on POLISH.

PROJECTS
  Same project format (version 9). Projects from 0.15 open in 0.16 as parts
  with their takes, stars and markers. Projects saved by 0.16 open in 0.15.
  Parts are the same thing as 0.15 "phrases". Part colours come from the
  name, so nothing new is stored.
