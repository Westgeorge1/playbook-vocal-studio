Playbook Vocal Studio 0.17.1 — changes since 0.17.0
====================================================

THE PROBLEM YOU REPORTED
  "When I click on record and start singing the recording stops by itself ... before I even finish the part."

WHY IT HAPPENED (root cause)
  A normal take (select a part -> big Record button / R) is recorded by the old 0.13 core. When the take started,
  the core set a timer for exactly the length of the part plus 0.3 s (pre-roll + part + 0.3 s) and stopped the
  recorder when it fired. It also scheduled the backing only up to that point, so the beat went quiet at the
  same moment. New parts are short (1–2 bars by default, about 3–6 s at 75 BPM), so the recording cut off
  mid-line. This has been the behaviour since 0.13; 0.17.0 made it the main way to record.
  Confirmed by a test on 0.17.0: with nobody pressing Stop, a take on a 3.2 s part ended at 3.5 s.

  Every other way a recording can end without you was checked too:
    - the microphone is unplugged / the browser's recorder fails  -> stops, with a message (unchanged)
    - punch-in: playback stopped at the end of the song (end of comp/backing)  -> now keeps going (see below)
    - punch-in / loop: the phone or browser paused the audio, or the app went to the background -> stops and
      keeps what was recorded, now with a message saying why
    - capacity checks (32 takes per part, 128 per project, 30 min of takes) happen before a take starts, not
      in the middle of it
  Only the part-length timer fired in the normal "select part -> press Record" flow.

WHAT CHANGED
  1. A take now records until YOU press Stop (R, Space, the big button or ■). It does not stop at the part's
     OUT flag. The backing keeps playing to its own end, and recording continues after the backing ends (a
     note says "The backing track has ended — still recording").
  2. Sang past OUT? The part grows to fit — up to the end of your singing (silence after your last note does
     not count, so a slow press of Stop does not make the part longer). One Undo puts the part and the take back.
       - If the longer part would run into the next part, you are asked:
           OK     = keep the next part as it is: this part ends where the next one starts, the take is trimmed there
           Cancel = make room: this part grows and the next part starts later (its end stays)
         Nothing is cut without asking.
       - A part can be at most 60 s long (a rule of the project format). If you keep singing longer, the rest
         goes into a new part right after it ("Verse 1 (cont.)") with that piece as its take — nothing is lost.
     The same applies to punch-in: a punch keeps going past OUT (the part grows) and even past the end of
     the song, until you punch out. Punching out after the song ended also stops the song.
  3. New switch on the part panel (desktop and phone): "⏹ Stop at part end: OFF / ON". OFF is the default.
     ON brings back the old behaviour: recording stops by itself just after OUT, and a punch-in punches out at
     OUT while the song keeps playing. It is saved with your other recording settings.
  4. Safety limit: one take can be at most 10 minutes (less only when the project is nearly full). You get a
     warning one minute before, and a message when it stops.
  5. Whenever recording stops without you pressing Stop, a plain message says why: "Stop at part end" is ON,
     safety limit, microphone unplugged, app went to the background, audio paused by the phone.
  6. Help text, "How it works", the empty-takes hint and RECORD-AND-TAKES.txt now describe this.
  7. Loop recording is unchanged: a take every full pass, and it loops until you stop it.

ALSO FIXED
  - The red "recording" area on the timeline now follows the take past OUT ("● still recording — the part will grow").
  - A take that is silent after the singing no longer stores the silent tail (saves memory on long takes).
  - A two-channel recording of a mono microphone (identical channels) is stored as one channel.

NOT CHANGED
  - Engine files byte-identical (phrase-core.js, worker.js, phrase-worker.js, engine-wasm.*, daw.css, rec-worklet.js).
  - Project format: 0.13–0.17.0 projects open in 0.17.1 and 0.17.1 projects open in 0.16.1/0.17.0
    (parts that grew are ordinary parts).
  - Everything else from 0.17.0.
