PLAYBOOK VOCAL STUDIO 0.22.0 — CHANGES (stage 2 of the 0.18.3 merge)
Built on 0.21.0. SongCore / SongStudio remain the only microphone owner. Engine files
(ENGINE-SHA256.txt) are byte-identical. Published to the beta link only.

PART FLOW FROM SECTIONS
- Part bar under Song sections: ◀ Previous part, the current part (time, take count,
  chosen), Next part ▶, ● Record part, Redo last take, Delete unchosen takes…,
  "Stop at part end" (OFF by default).
- Stop at part end OFF: recording runs until Stop; the part grows to fit the take, up to
  the start of the next part (the take itself is never cut). ON: recording stops by
  itself at the part end.
- The takes of a part are every recording on the armed track that starts at the
  section start. Choosing one take un-chooses the others in that part. The first good
  take of a part is chosen automatically; later takes wait for you to Choose.
- Redo last take removes the newest take of the part (Undo restores it) and records the
  part again. Delete unchosen keeps only the chosen take (asks first; Undo restores).
- Undo / Redo keep the current part selected.

TAKE STARS AND "WHY SUGGESTED?"
- 1–5 stars per take (click the same star again to clear), saved in the project.
- Every attempt is checked in the background with the 0.18.3 phrase-core analysis
  (phrase-worker.js, unchanged). The take list shows "check NN" per take and a
  Suggested badge when one take leads by 3 points or more. "Why suggested?" shows the
  score, pitch, timing, clipping and note for every take.
- A check belongs to the exact range, position, trim, fades and level of the take, so an
  edit, Undo or Redo runs it again (port of 0.18.3 review 06).

TAKE EDITOR
- Edit on a take: trim start, trim end, take level (dB), fade in, fade out, timing (ms),
  Reset. Non-destructive (the recording is never cut); Undo / Redo work. If an edit makes
  a chosen take too short for its range it is un-chosen and the app says so.
- ⋯ on a take keeps Rename, Timing and Remove.

START CARD, HELP, WITH / WITHOUT
- Empty session: start card with ● Record a vocal / Import a vocal / Open a project /
  + Add backing (hidden once a backing is loaded). It disappears once there is a vocal.
- The long how-to text is behind a Help link (top bar and start card).
- With / Without switch in the transport: hear the backing or not while you play and
  record. Without a loaded backing, With explains how to load one.

COMPATIBILITY WITH 0.18.3
- Project ZIPs also write 0.18.3 parts data (phoneRecording): the Lead vocal comp as the
  whole vocal and one part per section (plus fixes/recordings outside sections) with its
  takes, stars and chosen take. Parts longer than 60 s are split "(cont.)". Take edits
  are written as finished audio for 0.18.3. Verified by opening a 0.22 ZIP in the real
  0.18.3 app (tests-0.22/t22.py).
- 0.18.3 and earlier see only the Lead vocal and its parts. Backing up a project with
  more than one vocal track shows this warning; the Song project text on the export page,
  README, START-HERE and RECORD-AND-TAKES say the same.
- Opening an old project keeps its star ratings, check results, reference take, key and
  scale, and writes them back for 0.18.3.

POLISH TIDY
- One Undo / Redo pair (the note-editor duplicates are hidden; the transport pair is
  context-aware).
- One export entry per screen: Song & vocals → Export audio; POLISH → Export song, whose
  window also offers "Only <this vocal> (final, tuned, cleaned…)". The extra header
  export and the separate EXPORT THIS VOCAL buttons are hidden.
- 1024 px: the POLISH bar is one row (its help sentence is hidden below 1280 px), the
  app title no longer crowds the menu bar.
- 390 px: the POLISH bar sits under the step switch, the saved notice no longer floats
  over the lanes/notes.
- Phone Song screen: compact file bar, transport and section bar; zoom tools move below
  the timeline; Hear backing is on the first screen and the lane controls follow it.

DIALOGS
- Tab / Shift+Tab stay inside Song Studio dialogs (port of 0.18.3 review 08); Esc is the
  safe choice and focus returns to the opener.
