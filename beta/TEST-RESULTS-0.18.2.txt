HONEST HISTORY OF THE RUNS
  Run #1  all ok except: PH360 count-in (the two count-in boxes synced only on the next screen tick → fixed in
          rc.js, they now follow at once); audit (0.16 id rcBacking had no map entry → map added: With/Without +
          M); T182 at 1366 (the prepared-downloads box sat over the VOCAL ▾ menu → fixed in studio.css: it stays
          right of the lane headers and under menus); A1920 failed once (passed alone, 35/0).
  Run #2  T182 / T182W 74/0. run_all.out also showed two T182 FAIL lines that were STALE (an old t182.log left
          from an earlier debug run was picked up by the log scan) → the log was deleted and run_all.sh now
          scans only this run's logs. Real failures:
          - R172 real 125 s punch: test timing under load (the audio clock reached only 110 s in the 155 s the
            test waits, with 6 suites at once); the take itself ended exactly at the punch-out. Alone: 58/0.
          - OLD: page load timed out (30 s) under load. Alone: 14/0.
          - POL: Undo of a note edit: the engine needs about 1.8 s to apply it (0.17.2 needs 1.9 s, so not a
            regression); the test read it after a fixed 300 ms. The test now waits up to 8 s for the change and
            still checks the exact value. Alone: 24/0.
  Run #3  18 of 19 ok. D failed "50 BPM + 2-bar count-in + 300 ms latency" once. Alone 3 times: that check
          passed 3/3 (the take ends at 3.24 s for a part ending at 3.20 s — a tight margin, so the run #3 miss
          was load timing). But 2 of those 3 runs failed another check: "with 5 takes at 1366x768 the COMP lane
          is on screen". Cause: the laptop transport bar had only ~42 px to spare and could wrap to a second
          line when a readout got wider, which pushed the COMP lane out of view. Fixed (studio.css): the
          transport never wraps at 900 px and wider; the clock / tempo readouts shrink with "…" instead.
          After the fix: D 11/0 twice, t17 37/0. (No further full run was made after this CSS-only fix.)
