LIMITS
No listening preference or professional-release certification is claimed. Real vocals can expose pitch-detection errors and resynthesis artifacts that controlled tests miss. The user should choose the preset after hearing the supplied matched comparisons.
This release was tested in headless Chromium on Linux, including 1366/390px viewport checks. Physical Android/iOS, real microphone/headphone latency, low-memory behavior and background suspension were not tested.
The earlier review of the supplied ZIP stopped a long-stereo suite near the shared 8 GiB test-environment budget. It was not a pass. This release does not claim a fresh long-file capacity qualification from that interrupted run; the full real-vocal comparisons here are up to 52.80 seconds. Existing minimum sample-rate and stereo-frame limits remain.
