MANUAL CLEANUP · Playbook Vocal Studio 0.36.1 beta

On Song & vocals, tap the breath on the timeline and choose Clean up here. You can also press N, use a right-click or long press, or open Edit on the take and choose Manual cleanup. A short timeline selection is respected. At the end of a take you can open its final second. Use Select sound to mark audio, and Move view to scroll it.

Start with Reduce a breath. Drag across the breath or type Start and End. Find next possible breath suggests a location to verify by ear. Try the 6 dB starting point. Compare Before and After with the same backing setting. The surrounding words are included in both previews so you can hear whether the next consonant survives. Hear removed plays only the selected difference without the backing. Loop repeats the preview passage.

Keep as new take stores the edit and returns you to the song. Your source recording stays available. Song Undo restores the previous choice. Close leaves the edit uncommitted. Stop also cancels preparation. The action row stays visible. If a selection exceeds the local 30-second limit, shorten it or use Select one second.

For a low gust under a word, choose Rumble under voice. Advanced contains frequency limits, edge fade, the spectrum and separate WAV exports for the edited or removed selection. The starting rumble settings are 0–100 Hz and 4 dB. For steady room noise across a whole vocal, go to POLISH → CLEAN.
